Grammar Worksheet: Countable & Uncountable Nouns

7th Standard • English Grammar League

🏆 Part A: Categorization Challenge
Look at the underlined nouns in the sentences below. Select C if the noun is Countable, and U if it is Uncountable.
1. Dhira sat on a wooden box to shine shoes.
2. The witch poured boiling water into the big pot.
3. Ekalavya possessed immense courage and dedication.
4. The plastic bags filled the oceans and harmed marine life.
5. We need to buy three oranges from the market.
6. The old clock gave the lazy boy some good advice.
🔮 Part B: Quantity Match-Up
Choose the correct quantifier from the dropdown choice to complete each sentence smoothly.
1. There isn't ______________ milk left in the jug.
2. Dhira earned ______________ coins after polishing the thief's shoes.
3. How ______________ plastic garbage do we generate every day?
4. Dronacharya taught ______________ princes the art of archery.
5. Can you pass me ______________ advice on how to stay healthy?
🛠️ Part C: The "Unitizer" Transformation
Rewrite the sentences below by introducing a unit counter phrase (like "a glass of", "a slice of") for the underlined words.
1. The traveler drank cold water after the long journey.
2. Please put butter on my toast.
🚨 Part D: Error Detection
Identify the grammatical error concerning noun usage. Rewrite the complete sentence flawlessly below.
1. The teacher gave us many homeworks yesterday.
2. I saw a flock of beautiful infant deers near the forest.

🔑 Master Answer Key & Explanations

Part A: 1: C, 2: U, 3: U (Abstract), 4: C, 5: C, 6: U

Part B: 1: much, 2: a few, 3: much, 4: several, 5: a piece of

Part C Base Reference Answers:
1. "...drank a glass / bottle of cold water..."
2. "...put a pat / slice / dollop of butter..."

Part D Base Reference Answers:
1. "The teacher gave us a lot of homework yesterday." (Homework is uncountable).
2. "I saw a flock of beautiful infant deer near the forest." (Deer acts as both singular and plural).
