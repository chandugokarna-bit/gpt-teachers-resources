Kindness to Animals Studio

Anonymous Poem • Page 49-56 • Stanza-by-Stanza Parallel Interactive Learning

Little children, never give
Pain to things that feel and live.
Let the gentle robin come
For the crumbs you save at home.
As his meat you throw along
He'll repay you with a song.
Stanza 1 Care

🐦 The Gentle Robin's Reward

The poet requests little children to never hurt or cause pain to any living creatures because they can feel things just like us. Instead, we should welcome the gentle robin bird near our homes and feed it small food crumbs or scraps. In return for this small food, the robin will beautifully repay us by singing a lovely song.

Never hurt the timid hare
Peeping from her green grass lair.
Let her come and sport and play
On the lawn at close of day.
Stanza 2 Play

🐇 Freedom for the Timid Hare

The poet tells us never to frighten or harm the timid, easily scared hare (rabbit) when it peeps out from its hidden home in the green grass. We should let the peaceful creature come onto our lawns and gardens freely to jump, sport, and play happily at the end of the day when the sun sets.

The little lark goes soaring high
To the bright windows of the sky.
Singing as if 't were always spring
And fluttering on an untired wing
Oh! Let him sing his happy song
Nor do these gentle creatures wrong.
Stanza 3 Freedom

🎵 The High Flying Lark

The small lark bird flies incredibly high up into the bright, open sky. It flutters its untired wings and sings continuously as if it is always the beautiful spring season. The poet advises us to just let the bird sing its joyous tunes peacefully and never do anything wrong or cruel to these harmless, gentle creatures.
