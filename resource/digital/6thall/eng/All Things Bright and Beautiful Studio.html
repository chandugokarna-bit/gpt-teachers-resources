All Things Bright and Beautiful Studio

By Cecil Frances Alexander • Page 68-74 • Parallel Learning Interface

All things bright and beautiful,
All creatures great and small,
All things wise and wonderful:
The Lord God made them all.

Each little flower that opens,
Each little bird that sings,
He made their glowing colours,
He made their tiny wings.
Stanza 1 & 2 Creation

🌸 The Wonders of Nature

The poet celebrates that everything in this world that is bright, beautiful, wise, and wonderful—including all creatures, whether they are massive or tiny—is created entirely by Almighty God. God designed the beautiful, bright glowing colors of every opening flower and crafted the tiny, delicate wings of every sweet singing bird.

The purple headed mountains,
The river running by,
The sunset and the morning
That brightens up the sky.

The cold wind in the winter,
The pleasant summer sun,
The ripe fruits in the garden,
He made them every one.
Stanza 3 & 4 Landscape

⛰️ The Changing Seasons

The majestic mountains with their purple-looking tops, the freshwater rivers flowing continuously beside them, and both the sunset and sunrise that make the sky bright are all God's creations. God also thoughtfully made the cold wind that blows in the winter season, the pleasant sun of the summer, and the sweet ripe fruits hanging in the garden.

The tall trees in the greenwood,
The meadows where we play,
The rushes by the water,
To gather every day.

He gave us eyes to see them,
And lips that we might tell
How great is God Almighty,
Who has made all things well.
Stanza 5 & 6 Purpose

👁️ Purpose of Our Senses

God created the giant tall trees standing in the forests, the open grasslands (meadows) where children run and play, and the water plants (rushes) collected every day. Most importantly, God blessed humans with eyes so we can look at this beautiful nature, and lips so we can speak out loud and praise how great God Almighty is for making everything so perfectly.
