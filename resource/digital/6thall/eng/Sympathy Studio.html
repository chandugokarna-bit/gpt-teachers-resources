Sympathy Studio

By Charles Mackay • Stanza-by-Stanza Parallel Interactive Learning

I lay in sorrow, deep depressed...
My grief a proud man heard...
His looks were cold. He gave me gold.
But not a kindly word.
Stanza 1 Sarcasm

💰 The Coldness of Wealth

The poet was once in deep sadness, depression, and mental pain. A wealthy, proud man noticed his problem. Although the rich man gave him gold (money), his facial expression was very cold and unfriendly, and he did not offer a single soft, comforting, or kind word.

My sorrow passed - I paid him back
The gold he gave to me.
Then stood erect and spoke my thanks
And blessed his charity...
Stanza 2 Response

🧍 Debt Repaid with Dignity

When the poet's bad days came to an end and his sorrow passed away, he went straight back to the proud man. He returned every bit of the gold money he had received. Standing tall and straight with dignity, the poet expressed his deep gratitude and prayed for a blessing upon the man's financial help.

I lay in want, and grief and pain.
A poor man passed my way.
He bound my head. He gave me bread.
He watched me night and day.
Stanza 3 Kindness

🏡 True Human Compassion

Sometime later, the poet fell into trouble, hunger, and physical pain once more. This time, an ordinary poor man passed by him. Without hesitation, the poor man bandaged the poet's hurting head, fed him bread, and continuously looked after his safety during both night and day.

How shall I pay him back again
For all he did to me?
Oh! Gold is great. But greater far
Is heavenly sympathy!
Stanza 4 Moral

💖 Sympathy is Priceless

The poet ponders deeply and realizes he has no clue how to repay the poor man for his immense service and selfless care! He concludes that while money/gold is undoubtedly valuable, the divine emotion of **Sympathy** is infinitely superior and cannot be bought with all the wealth in the universe.
