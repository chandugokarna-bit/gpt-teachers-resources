📝 Activity 1: Simple Poem Summary

Read the summary of the poem in very easy sentences, broken down paragraph by paragraph.

Paragraph 1 (Stanza 1)
The poet was in deep sadness and pain[cite: 307]. A proud, rich man heard his cry[cite: 307]. The rich man gave him gold (money) but did not speak any kind or loving words to him[cite: 307, 308].
Paragraph 2 (Stanza 2)
When the poet's bad times passed, he went back and returned all the money to the proud man[cite: 308]. He stood straight, said "Thank You", and blessed the rich man's charity[cite: 309].
Paragraph 3 (Stanza 3)
Later, the poet fell into trouble, pain, and poverty again[cite: 309, 314]. This time, a poor man passed by him[cite: 310]. The poor man tied a bandage on his head, gave him bread to eat, and took care of him day and night[cite: 310].
Paragraph 4 (Stanza 4)
The poet wonders how he can ever pay back the poor man for his pure love[cite: 312]! Money is great, but a heavenly feeling like **Sympathy** is far greater than any gold in the world[cite: 312, 313]!
