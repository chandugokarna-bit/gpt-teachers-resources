The Rainbow Poem

By Christina Rossetti • Paragraph-Wise Parallel Learning Studio

Boats sail in the rivers,
And ships sail on the seas;
But clouds that sail across the sky,
Are prettier far than these.
Stanza 1 Summary

⛵ Earthly vs Heavenly Beauty

The poet says that man-made boats traveling on rivers and big ships traveling on the wide seas look very beautiful. However, natural clouds floating across the wide blue sky are much more beautiful than any boat or ship built by humans.

There are bridges on the rivers,
As pretty as you please;
But the bow that bridges heaven,
And overtops the trees,
And builds a road from earth to sky,
Is prettier far than these.
Stanza 2 Summary

🌈 The Road to Heaven

Human-built bridges over rivers look very neat and helpful. But a rainbow—which acts like a giant bridge to heaven, rises high over the tall green trees, and makes an imaginary colorful highway from the ground to the clouds—is vastly superior and more majestic than any human bridge.
