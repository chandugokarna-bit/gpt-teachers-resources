Stanza 1 — Gathering Honey

"How doth the little busy bee Improve each shining hour; And gather honey all the day From every opening flower!"

Stanza 2 — Skilled Architecture

"How skillfully she builds her cell! How neatly she spreads her wax, And labours hard to store it well With the sweet food she makes!"

Stanza 3 — Shunning Idleness

"In works of labour or of skill, I would be busy too; For Satan finds some mischief still For idle hands to do."

Stanza 4 — Accountability of Youth

"In books or work or healthful play, Let my first years be passed, That I may give for every day, Some good account at last."
