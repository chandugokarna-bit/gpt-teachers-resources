6th Century BCE

Causes for the Rise of New Religions

Due to the complex rituals, animal sacrifices, and rigid caste system prevalent in the Vedic religion, people desired a simpler path. This period witnessed the rise of Jainism and Buddhism.

c. 599 BCE

Birth of Vardhamana Mahavira

Mahavira was born in Kundagrama near Vaishali in Bihar. He is the 24th Tirthankara of Jainism. His father was Siddhartha and his mother was Trishala Devi.

c. 563 BCE

Birth of Gautama Buddha

Gautama Buddha was born in Lumbini, Nepal, as the son of King Shuddhodana and Queen Mayadevi of the Shakya clan. His childhood name was Siddhartha.

528 BCE

Enlightenment and First Sermon

Siddhartha attained enlightenment under the Bodhi tree in Gaya and became the 'Buddha'. He then delivered his first sermon at the Deer Park in Sarnath, an event known as 'Dharmachakra Pravartana' (Turning the Wheel of Dharma).

483 BCE

Mahaparinirvana

Gautama Buddha attained Mahaparinirvana (death) at Kushinagar, Bihar, at the age of 80. His teachings were later compiled and are known as the 'Tripitakas'.
