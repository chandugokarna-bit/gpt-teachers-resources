Linear Equations in One Variable

Welcome to your interactive math experience room! In Chapter 2, we dive into the wonderland of equations where a mysterious letter (like x or y) holds a hidden number value. Your mission is to use operations to set it free and discover its value!

Use the side menu bar to navigate through 5 custom-designed puzzle games. Solve them, click verify, and track your performance directly!

Activity 1: Spot the Linear Expression (MCQ)

Let's find out if you can spot a true Linear Equation in One Variable. The variable must have a maximum power of 1!

1. Which of the following is a linear equation in one variable?
2. What is the maximum power of the variable in a linear expression?
3. Why is "2x + 3y = 15" NOT an equation in one variable?
4. In the expression "5x - 3", what do we call the letter 'x'?
5. Which of these is a linear expression?
