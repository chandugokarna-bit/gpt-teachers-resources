Activity 2: Conceptual Group Questions (Click Box Activity)

Click on the highly crucial historical questions below to reveal and analyze their official answer boxes:

1. Why is the 18th century called the 'Period of Political Transition' in Indian history?
Because during this period, the powerful Mughal Empire collapsed, leading to the rise of several regional powers like the Marathas, Sikhs, and the Nizams of Hyderabad who became independent, thereby clearing the path for Europeans to assume administrative control.
2. How did the Third Battle of Panipat alter the political landscape of India?
In this battle fought in 1761 CE, Ahmad Shah Abdali decisively defeated the Marathas. This shattered the Marathas' dream of pan-Indian hegemony and made it easier for the British to capture India.
3. How did the rise of Hyder Ali occur in the history of Mysore?
Hyder Ali joined the court of the Wodeyar kings of Mysore as an ordinary soldier. Through his exceptional bravery and astuteness, he rose to become the 'Dalaway' (commander), and eventually became the de facto ruler of Mysore in 1761 CE.
4. Why is Tipu Sultan referred to as the 'Tiger of Mysore' in history?
Tipu was a ruler who fought fiercely against British imperialism without making any compromises. He is highly acclaimed for his valor, military innovations during the Anglo-Mysore wars, and for achieving martyrdom on the battlefield.
5. What was the achievement of Maharaja Ranjit Singh, who built the highly influential Sikh Empire?
Ranjit Singh unified the scattered Sikh Misls (clans) to establish a formidable Sikh Empire. He organized a modern army on the European model, which deeply alarmed both the Afghans and the British.
6. What internal weaknesses led to the decline of the Mughal Empire in the 18th century?
The weak rulers who succeeded Aurangzeb, internal conspiracies among court nobles, continuous wars of succession, and economic bankruptcy brought about the downfall of the Mughal Empire.
