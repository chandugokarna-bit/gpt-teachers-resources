Activity 2: Analytical Group Discussion Questions (Click Box Activity)

Click on the key historical questions below to open and analyze their official answer boxes:

1. What were the diplomatic conspiracies hatched by the British to defeat the Marathas?
Taking advantage of the internal conflicts among the Maratha leaders, the British supported Raghunath Rao and broke the Maratha confederacy through the Treaty of Salbai and the Treaty of Bassein to establish their supremacy.
2. How did the Subsidiary Alliance system victimize the Indian native rulers?
Due to this system, Indian rulers lost their own military strength, had to keep a British Resident in their court, and could not enter into any foreign agreement without British permission. This took away their complete independence.
3. How did the British annex the Sikh Empire?
After the death of Maharaja Ranjit Singh, anarchy broke out in the Sikh Empire. Taking advantage of this, the British annexed the entire Punjab to the British Empire through the Treaty of Lahore and successive Anglo-Sikh wars.
4. What was the main objective of the 'Doctrine of Lapse' policy?
The main objective was that if any native ruler did not have a natural heir (male child), the British would not recognize any adopted child, and such states would be directly merged into the British Empire.
5. What were the political consequences of the Third Anglo-Maratha War?
With this war, the British completely abolished the post of Peshwa, granted him a pension, and sent him to Bithoor. Pratap Simha, a descendant of Shivaji, was made the ruler of the small state of Satara.
6. How did the Regulating Act and Pitt's India Act control British administration?
Through these acts, the British government imposed direct control over the political and commercial affairs of the East India Company in India and granted greater administrative powers to the post of Governor-General.
