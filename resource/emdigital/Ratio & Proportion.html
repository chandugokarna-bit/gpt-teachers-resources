1. Mastering Ratios through Scale Comparisons

A Ratio is a mathematical comparison of two or more quantities of the same kind using division. It represents how many times one value contains another.

2. Proportion Equality & Cross Multiplication

When two ratios are declared equal, they form a Proportion. If a:b = c:d, then the product of the extremes equals the product of the means (a × d = b × c).

3. Direct Proportion & Linear Scaling Graphs

Two variables are in Direct Proportion if an increase in one causes a constant proportionate increase in the other. Formally, y / x = k where k is the constant of variation.

🧠 Brainstorm Challenge: Shadows at Sundown

4. Inverse Proportion & Hyperbolic Resource Distributions

In an Inverse Proportion, as one variable increases, the other decreases such that their mathematical product remains constant: x × y = k.
