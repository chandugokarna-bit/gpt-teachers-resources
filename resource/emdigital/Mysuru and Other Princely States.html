Activity 2: Critical Group Discussion Questions (Click Box Activity)

Click on the prominent historical questions below to unfold and analyze their official answer boxes:

1. Why is the establishment of the 'Representative Assembly' a milestone in the history of Mysuru?
Established in 1881 during the period of Diwan Rangacharlu, the Representative Assembly was the first of its kind among India's princely states to grant citizens a voice in administration, serving as an early harbinger of democracy.
2. What is the significance of Sir M. Visvesvaraya's slogan 'Industrialize or Perish'?
Realizing that Mysuru could not grow economically if it relied solely on agriculture, Visvesvaraya established the Bhadravati Iron Works, Mysore Sandal Soap Factory, and the State Bank of Mysore, turning Mysuru into an industrial hub.
3. Why did Gandhiji call Nalwadi Krishnaraja Wadiyar a 'Rajarshi' (Royal Saint)?
Nalwadi Krishnaraja Wadiyar implemented reservations for backward classes (Miller Committee Report), introduced compulsory free primary education, abolished the Devadasi system, and granted voting rights to women, leading Gandhiji to praise Mysuru as a model state.
4. What were the contributions of Diwan Sir K. Seshadri Iyer to the modern development of Mysuru?
He initiated Asia's first hydroelectric power project at Shivanasamudra across the Kaveri river (1902), supplied power to the Kolar Gold Fields (KGF), and planned the residential extensions of Chamarajapet and Malleshwaram in Bengaluru.
5. How did the valor of Madakari Nayaka and Obavva become immortal among the Palegars of Chitradurga?
When Hyder Ali besieged the Chitradurga Fort, the brave ruler Madakari Nayaka fought with immense courage. Simultaneously, a courageous common woman named Obavva used a wooden pestle (Onake) to slay enemy soldiers entering through a secret crevice before achieving martyrdom.
6. How did aesthetics and industry grow together during the era of Diwan Mirza Ismail?
Mirza Ismail constructed the world-famous Brindavan Gardens near the KRS Dam, transforming Mysuru into a garden city. He also vastly expanded Khadi village industries and sugar factories.
