Amazing Characteristics of Living Organisms 🌱✨

Growth

Living organisms change in size and shape over time. E.g., A small seed becoming a big tree.
🫁

Respiration

To obtain energy from food, living organisms take in oxygen and release carbon dioxide.
🏃

Movement

Animals move for food and protection. Plants bend toward light.
🐣

Reproduction

They continue their lineage by giving birth to new organisms of their own species.
⚡

Response

Living organisms respond to environmental changes such as light, sound, or touch.
🗑️

Excretion

The process of eliminating waste materials produced in the body.
