Activity 2: Conceptual Group Questions (Click Box Activity)

Click on the key descriptive questions below to open and study their accurate answer frameworks:

1. What was the main circumstance that led to the discovery of a new sea route to India?
When the Ottoman Turks captured the city of Constantinople in 1453 CE, the traditional land route used by Europeans was closed. This made finding a new sea route unavoidable.
2. Between whom was the Battle of Plassey fought and what was its result?
It was fought in 1757 CE between the Nawab of Bengal, Siraj-ud-Daulah, and Robert Clive of the British. Due to the treachery of Mir Jafar, the British emerged victorious.
3. Why holds the Battle of Buxar immense significance in history?
In this battle fought in 1764 CE, the British defeated the combined forces of three Indian rulers. This secured the real 'Diwani' rights (revenue collection rights) over Bengal for the British, consolidating their hegemony.
4. Who introduced the system of 'Dual Government' and what did it mean?
Robert Clive introduced this system. Under this, while the British held the right to collect land revenue (Diwani), the Nawab retained the responsibility for general administration and the maintenance of justice.
5. What was the primary objective of the British 'Subsidiary Alliance System'?
The primary objective of this system brought by Lord Wellesley was to bring Indian princely states under British sovereignty and to completely curtail French political influence in India.
6. Which major states were annexed into the British Empire due to the 'Doctrine of Lapse'?
Under this policy introduced by Lord Dalhousie, states like Satara, Jaipur, Sambalpur, Udaipur, Jhansi, and Nagpur were annexed by the British.
