Activity 2: Group Discussion Questions (Click Box Activity)

Click on the key historical questions below to reveal and analyze their official answer boxes:

1. What was the primary social context that led to the rise of the Bhakti Movement?
The common people in medieval Indian society were exhausted by the rigid caste system, untouchability, elaborate and expensive religious rituals, and superstitions. The Bhakti Movement emerged to proclaim that everyone is equal before God.
2. How did Kabir Das's 'Dohas' foster social harmony in society?
Through his simple couplets (Dohas), Kabir condemned the external rituals and hypocrisy of both Hinduism and Islam. He proclaimed that Allah and Rama are one and the same, thereby instilling religious harmony among the people.
3. What are the core teachings of Guru Nanak, the founder of Sikhism?
Guru Nanak preached monotheism (the oneness of God). He strongly opposed the caste system and brought equality to society by introducing congregational prayers (Sangat) and a free community kitchen (Langer).
4. What is the contribution of Purandara Dasa and Kanaka Dasa to the Haridasa tradition of Karnataka?
Purandara Dasa corrected societal hypocrisy through his devotional songs (Keertanas) and became known as the 'Father of Carnatic Music'. Kanaka Dasa strongly opposed caste discrimination through his metaphorical poems (Mundiges) and Keertanas, proclaiming 'Do not fight over caste, lineage, and creed'.
5. What are the main features and teachings of the Sufi tradition?
Sufi saints believed that God could be reached not through rigid rules, but through pure love and devotion. They attained spiritual ecstasy through music and dance (Sama) and advocated that service to humanity is the supreme religion.
6. Why is Shishunala Sharifa referred to as the 'Kabir of Karnataka'?
Born into a Muslim community, Sharifa became a disciple of the Hindu guru Govinda Bhatta and gained spiritual knowledge. Through his philosophical songs (Tatvapadas), he awakened Hindu-Muslim unity. Just like Kabir, he corrected social inequalities, which earned him this title.
