Clinical Thermometer
  • Range: 35°C to 42°C only.
  • Kink: It contains a small kink to prevent the mercury level from falling down on its own.
  • Use: Only for measuring human body temperature. It must be shaken before reuse.
Laboratory Thermometer
  • Range: -10°C to 110°C.
  • No Kink: There is no kink in it, so the mercury drops down automatically as soon as the temperature decreases.
  • Use: For measuring the temperature of liquids/objects in the laboratory (e.g., when water boils).
